# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Artwork Creator is a desktop application made by bzhmaddog. This policy explains
what the application does with your data.

## Data collection

Artwork Creator does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No telemetry, analytics, crash reports or usage statistics are sent.
- No advertising and no third-party tracking.
- The application does not connect to the internet.

## Data stored on your computer

The application keeps the following on your own computer only:

- the projects, images and exports you create, where you choose to save them;
- your settings (preferences, keyboard shortcuts, print settings);
- recovery copies of open projects (autosave), so work can be restored after a crash;
- templates and plugins you add.

These files never leave your computer unless you share them yourself. Uninstalling
the application and deleting its data folder removes them.

## Plugins

Plugins are installed by you and run on your computer. Third-party plugins are not
covered by this policy; check with their authors what they do.

## Children

The application does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address
with an updated date.
