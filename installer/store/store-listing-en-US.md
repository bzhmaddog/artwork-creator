## Description

Artwork Creator is a desktop app for designing printed artwork for arcade controllers and fightsticks.

Load a controller template (an outline and holes), arrange your images, texts and shapes on top, and export print-ready PNG, PDF or SVG at true size. The artwork is clipped to the panel outline and left blank in the button and cutout holes, so what you print fits your controller.

Layers and groups of images, texts (straight or along a path), vector shapes, SVG paths and canvases to paint in, with clipping masks, blend modes and opacity. Solid colors, gradients and image patterns, and a stack of effects per layer, extended by plugins.

Move, scale, rotate, distort and tilt in 3D on the canvas, with snapping to the template's holes, the page, the outline and guides. Variables and formulas link sizes, positions, colors and texts.

A template editor lets you make your own templates, with checks, versions and a reference drawing to trace.

Export to PNG, PDF and SVG (vectors kept), or print, with bleed, crop marks, mirroring and tiling over several sheets.

Autosave and crash recovery, configurable shortcuts and movable panels. In English and French.

Free for non-commercial use. Making artwork, panels or controllers for sale needs the author's permission (see the license).
