Artwork Creator

Design printed artwork for arcade controllers and fightsticks.

Download Source on GitHub

Windows and Linux · Free for non-commercial use

Load a controller template drawn in SVG (an outline and holes), arrange your images on top, and export print-ready PNG, PDF or SVG at true size. The artwork is clipped to the panel outline and left blank in the button and cutout holes.

Features

  • Layers and groups of images, texts (typed on the canvas or along a path), vector shapes, SVG paths and canvases to paint in, with clipping masks, blend modes and opacity.
  • Solid colors, gradients, image patterns and generated fills; a stack of effects per layer.
  • Plugins for more effects and fills, native or in Python.
  • Move, scale, rotate, distort and tilt in 3D, with snapping to the template's holes, the page, the outline and guides.
  • Variables and formulas to link sizes, positions, colors and texts.
  • A template editor, with checks, versions and a reference drawing to trace; projects keep a copy of their template, so they open the same anywhere.
  • Export to PNG, PDF and SVG, and printing, with bleed, marks, mirroring and tiling, and previews of the cuts, bleed and safe area on the canvas.
  • Autosave and crash recovery, configurable shortcuts, English and French.
